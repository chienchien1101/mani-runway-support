Mani Runway Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Mani Runway ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our Application.

Data Storage

All financial data (including assets, liabilities, and expense schedules) entered in the Application is stored locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or share your data to any external server or third party.

Stock Price and Exchange Rate Data

The Application fetches public market data (such as Taiwan Stock Exchange and Frankfurter Exchange Rate API) to perform asset calculations. These API queries do not transmit any personally identifiable information.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
