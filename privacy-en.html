Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Mani Runway ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App, Premium subscription, and related services.

Information We Handle

Assets, liabilities, payment schedules, settings, and net-worth snapshots that you enter are stored locally on your device by default. We do not sell your personal financial content or use it for advertising tracking.

When you use a Premium subscription, Apple App Store processes payment. RevenueCat acts on behalf of the App to pass the subscription status, purchase records, and restoration information necessary to verify Premium access. RevenueCat does not receive your Apple ID password or complete payment details.

Third-Party Services and Market Data

The App may request stock prices or exchange rates from public sources such as the Taiwan Stock Exchange and exchange-rate services. Requests may include a ticker symbol, query date, and standard network request information, but the App does not intentionally attach your financial content or identity information.

Apple, RevenueCat, and other third-party services process information under their own privacy policies. Please review their official policies for more information.

Use, Retention, and Deletion

We use necessary information to provide App features, verify Premium access, process restoration, maintain service security, and respond to support requests. On-device financial data remains until you delete it from the App or device. You may clear local data in the App; clearing it may be irreversible, so keep your own backup of important information.

Contact Us

For questions about this policy or data handling, email manirunway.app@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when features or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
